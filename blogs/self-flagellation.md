title: live life fully
date: 2026-05-19
---

*You are the sum of your WHOLE life, not your professional accomplisments - John McEnroe.*

I've recently realized I was wrong about a fundamental issue. I believed that suffering was nessecary for success. That one needed to be in as much discomfort, stress, and emotional turmoil in order to reap the benefits of an award. And, I see now that is wrong. There is a strong culture that delayed gratification will lead to a better life, that your current misery will lead to later prosperity. But, I believe it comes to a point. So, what if your salary is marginally better in the future. Life is full of trade offs and your prioritization of wealth may not be important when you miss your child's birthday or see a loved one. Don't delay living life, you only get one shot.
